Optional settings that go with a theme.

## Cursor Effect

**Cursor effect** adds an optional pointer effect: **Off**, **Sandevistan** or **Comet**.

{{< shot src="images/cursor-effect.png" alt="The Cursor effect section" width="400" >}}

## Sign-in Page

**Choose a picture…** puts your own picture behind the sign-in box; the *Sign-in* tab of the preview shows it. The picture is kept with the theme as it is (an SVG, or a file over 10 MB, is made into a WebP at most 1920 px wide). **Use the web UI's picture** takes it away again.

![A chosen picture in the Sign-in page section and in the Sign-in scene](/images/sign-in-page.png)

## All Tokens

**All tokens** lists every color the web UI has, for when you want one exact value. Point at one and the preview outlines what it paints, as it does for a color's row.

{{< shot src="images/all-tokens.png" alt="All tokens filtered by the word accent" width="400" >}}
