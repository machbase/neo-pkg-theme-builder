The preview draws the web UI in your colors as you change them.

## Six Scenes

Each tab says how many colors it holds.

- **Workspace**: the left bar, tabs, side panel, editor, results, console
- **Explorer**: the side panel's lists — the DB explorer and the packages — and a table's page
- **New tab**: with Customize open; its Widgets panel is a drawer
- **Dialogs**: a dialog over its backdrop, inputs, buttons, a menu, a tooltip
- **Status**: badges, status dots, messages, notices, log levels, a tab that failed to draw, a toast
- **Sign-in**: the sign-in page

## See What a Color Paints

Point at a color's row and the preview shows a scene it is in, with the parts it paints outlined, and the tabs of every scene it shows in light up. Click any part of the preview to jump to its color.

![Pointing at Accent: the parts it paints are outlined and the scene tabs light up](/images/preview-outline.png)

## Contrast Checks

The contrast checks under the preview warn you when text becomes hard to read.

![The contrast checks after making Content light: the text that no longer reads is marked LOW](/images/contrast-checks.png)
